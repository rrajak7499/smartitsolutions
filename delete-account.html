Account & Data Deletion

Request deletion of your account and associated data.

How to delete your account

Option 1 – In the app: open the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2 – Request here: submit the form with the email address linked to your account and the app name. We will verify your request and process it.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile picture).
  • Content and settings stored on our servers.
  • Device identifiers associated with your account.

What may be retained

Limited records may be kept where required by law (for example, security logs or legal obligations) for up to 90 days, after which they are deleted.

Timeline

We process requests within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation when deletion is complete.
